1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy outlines how NEWSROOM ("we," "our," or "the App") collects, uses, maintains, and discloses information gathered from users ("User" or "you") of the NEWSROOM Android mobile application available on the Google Play Store.

We are committed to protecting your personal privacy and adhering strictly to the Google Play Developer Distribution Agreement and Developer Program Policies regarding User Data. NEWSROOM is built on an editorial-first foundation: we do not sell your personal data or participate in intrusive ad-tracking networks.

2. Information We Collect

We collect limited information to provide, personalize, and improve your news delivery experience:

A. Information You Voluntarily Provide

  • Newsletter Subscription: When you subscribe to "The Daily Brief" or "Pakistan Morning Memo," you provide your email address. This is used solely to deliver verified editorial newsletters you have requested.
  • Followed Topics & Preferences: Topics you follow (e.g., Pakistan, Diplomacy, Artificial Intelligence, Markets) and articles you bookmark for offline reading are saved locally on your device to personalize your feed.
  • Editorial CMS Inputs (If Authorized): Content, drafts, and dispatch data entered via the BhullarTech CMS editor are stored to publish updates to your local and synced newsroom streams.

B. Automatically Collected Technical Information

  • Device & Network State: To deliver real-time news headlines, the App checks connectivity (ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE and INTERNET) to fetch latest feeds via encrypted HTTPS connections.
  • Non-Personal Diagnostic Data: Aggregated, anonymized performance metrics (such as crash logs and HTTP response status) to ensure app reliability and speedy rendering.

Zero Sensitive Device Access

NEWSROOM does NOT request or access sensitive device permissions such as exact GPS location, contacts, camera, microphone, device storage files, SMS, or phone state.

3. Third-Party Services & APIs

The App integrates with reputable external services strictly to retrieve and display news content:

  • GNews API: Used to syndicate verified global top-headlines and regional Pakistani news dispatches via secure, encrypted REST calls.
  • Coil & Image CDNs (Unsplash): Used to load high-resolution editorial photography and photojournalism accompanying dispatches. Images are cached locally on device memory to reduce bandwidth usage.

These third-party providers process requests in accordance with their respective privacy policies. They do not receive any user identification data from NEWSROOM.

4. How We Use Collected Information

Collected information is used exclusively to:

  • Fetch, render, and update global and Pakistani news articles, breaking tickers, and live timelines.
  • Deliver requested editorial newsletters ("The Daily Brief").
  • Remember your bookmarked articles and followed topics for offline reading.
  • Analyze aggregated reading trends and top-performing news desks inside the BhullarTech CMS analytics view.
  • Protect the application against malicious traffic or abuse.

5. Data Storage, Security & Retention

Encryption in Transit: All network communication between the App and external news syndication servers is conducted using industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS).

Local Device Storage: Your reading history, bookmarked articles, and topic selections reside in private application sandbox storage on your device and are never broadcast to third-party ad networks.

Data Retention: Newsletter subscription emails are retained only for as long as you maintain an active subscription. You may unsubscribe or request removal at any time.

In compliance with international data protection standards and Google Play Console requirements, you have the right to:

  • Access & Review: View all saved articles and followed topics directly within the "My News" tab.
  • Clear Data: Clear your reading history and saved bookmarks directly in the App or by clearing App Data via Android System Settings.
  • Account / Email Deletion: To request complete deletion of any submitted email addresses or newsletter subscriptions, please submit a deletion request to:

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately, and we will promptly remove such information.
